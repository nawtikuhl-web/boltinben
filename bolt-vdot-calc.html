Flat route or track. Warm up 10 minutes, then go all out for one mile. No saving anything for later.

VDOT score, training paces, race predictions, and heart rate zones. Retest every 4–8 weeks to track real progress.
Quick safety note
This is an all-out maximal effort. Before attempting, make sure you're injury-free and have no cardiovascular conditions that high-intensity running could aggravate. If anything's off, see a doctor first. Run this test at your own risk.
