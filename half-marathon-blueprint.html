Who this is for: runners who can currently run 5–10km comfortably and want a structured path to their first or fastest half marathon. You need consistency and about 4–5 hours a week.
How The Plan Works

Phase 1 — Base

Weeks 1–4. Build your aerobic engine. Easy mileage, Zone 2 focus, long runs. Nothing heroic. Just consistent.

Phase 2 — Build

Weeks 5–9. Add quality sessions — tempo runs, intervals. Long runs extend to 16–18km. This is where fitness compounds.

Phase 3 — Taper

Weeks 10–12. Volume drops, intensity stays. Your body consolidates the work. Trust the taper.

The Golden Rules
  • 📌80% of your runs should feel easy. If you are breathing hard on easy days, you are going too fast.
  • 📌The long run is non-negotiable. Miss a tempo, make it up. Miss a long run — do not try to cram it in.
  • 📌Sleep is training. 7–9 hours is part of the plan, not a bonus.
  • 📌If something hurts (not soreness — actual pain), take a day.
  • 📌On runs over 75 min: fuel every 30–40 min. Practice in training — nothing new on race day.

Session Glossary

Every session type explained.

Easy Run

Zone 2. Full sentences. Nose breathing ideally. Do not run these faster than prescribed.

Tempo Run

Comfortably hard. Sustained effort 20–40 min. Raises your lactate threshold.

Intervals 800m

Hard repeats, easy jog recovery. 5x800m at 5k effort, 90 sec between. Builds VO2max.

Intervals 1600m

Longer repeats at 10k effort. More race-specific. 2 full minutes recovery.

Race Pace Run

Sustained effort at goal race pace. Teaches your body what that pace feels like.

Long Run

Always easy. Time on feet, not speed. Fat adaptation and mental durability.

Strides

6–8x 20-second accelerations to 90% effort, full recovery. Keeps legs sharp.

Deload Week

Cut volume 30%. Keep some quality, drop the long run. You get stronger during recovery.

Race Day Guide

Nothing is new — you have trained for this.

The cardinal rule: nothing new on race day. Not shoes, nutrition, socks, or warmup routine. Trust it.
  • 🌙
    Night before: Lay out every item. Normal dinner. Bed on time. Two alarms.
  • ☀️
    Morning: Eat 2–3 hours out. 500ml water on waking. Coffee only if you are used to it.
  • 🏃
    Warmup: 10–15 min easy jog plus dynamic stretches. Skip static stretching pre-race.
  • 🚀
    First 5km: Start 10–15 sec/km slower than race pace. The race starts at km 15.
  • 🍬
    Nutrition: First gel at km 5–6, then every 30–40 min. Wash down with water.
  • 💧
    Aid stations: Walk through them. 10 seconds beats choking and losing 90 seconds.
  • 🧠
    Km 15 onwards: Break into 2km chunks. Cadence, relaxed shoulders, forward lean.
  • 🏁
    Finish: Leave nothing in the tank. The last kilometre is yours.
Post-Race Recovery
  • 🥤Within 30 min: protein plus carbs. Recovery shake, banana plus milk.
  • 🛁Day 1–2: walk, gentle movement, contrast shower. No running.
  • 😴Day 3–5: prioritise sleep. Your body is doing the hard work now.
  • 📅Return to running: easy only after 7–10 days.
