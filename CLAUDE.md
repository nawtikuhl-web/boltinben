# Boltin Ben site: design rules

From Ben's brand guide and "never use" sheet (25 Nov 2026). They apply to
every page, whatever the colours.

## Never use

- A single border on one edge of a card or tile (left, right, top or bottom accent line).
- Pulsing dot eyebrows, or a dot inside a bordered pill.
- An em dash before an eyebrow, or a short accent line above a header.
- Emoji in place of icons.
- A serif or script word for emphasis inside a headline.
- Pagination footers on cards ("01 / 02" with a rule above).
- Bebas Neue or Instrument Serif.
- Em dashes anywhere in copy.

## Type and colour

- Barlow throughout. Avoid super bold: headlines are Barlow semibold, uppercase.
- Eyebrows are Barlow medium. Body copy is Barlow regular, sentence case.
- Mono is only for numbers on their own, or on graphs.
- Colours: #F5E215 yellow, #A78BFB purple, #8BFBB4 mint, #000000, #FFFFFF.
- Arrows are the plain "→" character.

## Layout

- Photos sit behind the text with the subject kept in focus; text large enough for phones.
- Simple infographics for key facts: rounded bars, rings and pies in the brand colours. A glass panel over a photo is fine.
- End sections can use a portrait of Ben.
