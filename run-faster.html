The ones people ask.

What if I'm slower than I think I am?

You are. Everyone is on day 1. That's why day 1 exists. The number is the starting line, not a verdict, and in 30 days it's the one you beat.

Will the easy runs feel like a waste of time?

Yes, for about a week. Then the session comes and you'll understand what they were for. Easy days are the injury prevention plan. There isn't a separate one.

I'm 40. Am I too old to get faster?

No. You're too old to do the volume you did at 25, which is why every comeback has ended in a calf. This plan doesn't ask for that volume.

I can't run 5K yet.

Then this one isn't for you yet, and I'd rather tell you now. The Base Builder Bible starts from zero. Finish that and this is day 31.

Do I need a watch?

No. The plan is cards and a phone timer.

What if I miss a session? I have kids.

Skip it. Don't stack it onto the next day. Missing an easy run changes nothing. Missing the session, do that one next and drop an easy. The plan is built for a week where something goes wrong.

Is it safe for me?

If you can run 5K now and aren't carrying an injury, yes. The session is hard but short, and three of the four runs are easier than what you do now. If you have a condition or aren't sure, check with your doctor first.

Why 30 days?

Long enough for a real adaptation, short enough that work and family don't get a chance to cancel it. And it's what you do before a 10K or half block so the block doesn't break you.
